A task completion does not create readiness or a job. |

| 4:10–5:00 | Return to **LAX → Overview → Weekly review**. Expand one calculation and open its evidence. | Reconcile target and actual, the stated comparison, current constraint, accountable action and actual next review date. Explain unavailable comparisons. The review follows the selected market and current snapshot; task due dates and goal deadlines are not silently treated as review dates. |
